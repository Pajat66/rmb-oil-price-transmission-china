# Final Data Availability Statement for v2.0.0

The research package for this study, version v2.0.0, is available at https://github.com/Pajat66/rmb-oil-price-transmission-china and is archived in Zenodo at https://doi.org/[V2_VERSION_DOI]. It contains the revised Python analysis and plotting code, data dictionary, source and transformation specifications, sample definitions and registries, saved author-generated statistical results and plotted statistical data, seven main figures, six main tables, 18 Supporting Tables, three Supporting Figures, and the FRED EXCHUS observation extract. Author-owned software is licensed under MIT; author-original figures, tables and shareable statistical outputs are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Third-party observations retain their providers' terms and are not relicensed by the authors.

The underlying macroeconomic observations cover January 1998–February 2026. Brent prices are the US Energy Information Administration's Europe Brent Spot Price FOB, monthly series RBRTE, in USD/barrel (https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=RBRTE&f=M). Exchange rates are the Federal Reserve Board's monthly EXCHUS series, in CNY/USD, accessed through FRED (https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/EXCHUS); its 338-month extract is included with attribution. National Bureau of Statistics of China observations are available through National Data (https://data.stats.gov.cn/), selecting national monthly data for CPI, industrial producer prices, total industrial purchase prices and fuel-and-power purchase prices (year-on-year indices, previous-year same month = 100), and monthly and cumulative industrial value-added growth. DATA_SOURCES.md specifies the exact Chinese indicator labels, date batches, units, missing-value conventions and download procedures; DATA_DICTIONARY.md and config/raw_inputs.json identify the variables and historical input checksums.

Monthly crude-oil import quantities and values for January 2015–February 2026 (134 months) were obtained from the General Administration of Customs statistical query platform (https://stats.customs.gov.cn/): imports, HS27090000, monthly grouping, RMB amounts in yuan and first legal quantity in kilograms, without specific partner, trade-mode or registration-region subgroup selections for the intended national series. Author-supplied official self-service exports for 2015 and 2016 were independently compared with the historical research workbook: all 24 monthly quantities and all 24 RMB amounts matched exactly. An additional historical-service application is not a prerequisite for these verified two years. The remaining 110 months were not newly exported and compared in that check; the repository documents the corresponding monthly retrieval procedure without claiming a new 134-month official comparison.

The package does not redistribute the other original provider exports or complete mixed monthly observation tables. EIA's historical Brent file credits Thomson Reuters, and its reuse policy excepts protected private contributions (https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php). NBS terms restrict unauthorized onward licensing (https://www.stats.gov.cn/wzgl/202302/t20230217_1912857.html; scope enquiries: info@stats.gov.cn). A file-specific right to redistribute the Customs exports has not been established. Researchers should obtain these observations from the official query/download routes above, subject to provider terms; for a genuinely unavailable Customs vintage or access/permission question, the official statistical-service procedure is https://online.customs.gov.cn/static/pages/guides/002029004002/002029004002.html. No author-only data-access permission or special access privilege is required by this package. Historical checksums distinguish the study vintage; subsequent provider revisions must be documented rather than silently substituted.

Figure 1 is a workflow diagram. Figure 2 requires the individual monthly observations, including displayed outliers, calendar coverage and equation-specific eligibility masks. RMB oil cost is Brent multiplied by CNY/USD; oil movements are 100 times adjacent calendar-month log differences; inflation is the year-on-year index minus 100, and the stationary outcomes are adjacent calendar-month changes in that inflation measure. Customs unit values are RMB yuan divided by kilograms. Missing months, transformation-edge months, two lags and horizon-specific leads must be preserved, as specified in the retained code, S17 Table, registries and docs/FIGURE_DATA_ACCESS.md. The complete mixed monthly Figure 2 source is obtained by applying these rules to provider observations and is not redistributed here; its TIFF or boxplot summaries do not replace the necessary observations. Frozen plotted statistics for Figures 3–7 and S1–S3 Figs are included in figures/source and the related saved-result directories. Full numerical and rendering reproduction was not newly executed during release preparation.
