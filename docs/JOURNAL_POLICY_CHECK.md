# Journal requirements and data-access limits

The following summarizes the recorded 2026-10-14 review of official PLOS ONE [data availability](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability), [Supporting Information](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/supporting-information), [figures](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/figures/), [licences and copyright](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/licenses-and-copyright) and [submission guidelines](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/submission-guidelines). It is a dated policy review, not a new policy or rights assessment or certification of editorial acceptance.

The minimal dataset includes observations needed for the findings plus methods and metadata. For third-party observations that cannot be distributed, source identification, applicable terms, access procedures/contacts and known restrictions must be described. The package includes the public-domain EXCHUS extract; EIA/NBS/Customs original exports and complete mixed monthly observations remain excluded. [Official source instructions](../DATA_SOURCES.md), [the access matrix](../DATA_ACCESS_MATRIX.csv) and [third-party exceptions](../licenses/THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md) retain the object-specific limits. NBS conditional quotation does not establish an unrestricted dataset-deposit grant. The recorded review states that a directly deposited dataset must not carry terms more restrictive than CC BY; provider acquisition or specific permission clarification remains the applicable route for the excluded observations.

Earlier official 2015–2016 Customs exports matched 24 months and 48 quantity/RMB integers. This does not establish fresh official equality for the remaining 110 months, exact current availability of every historical vintage, or permission to redistribute raw exports. Figure 2 requires the individual monthly observations and outliers; [its data-access document](FIGURE_DATA_ACCESS.md) preserves the incomplete and untested portable reconstruction boundary. No concrete necessary observation without a documented provider route was identified in that review; the instructions are not proof of complete fresh-data recovery.

The recorded main-figure requirements were TIFF/EPS, 300–600 dpi, maximum 10 MB and specified dimensions; at 300 dpi, width 789–2250 pixels and height at most 2625 pixels. The seven supplied TIFFs passed the earlier measured format/dimension checks; [figure reproduction](FIGURE_REPRODUCTION.md) records actual exports and verification scope. The guidelines reviewed waived main-figure formatting until provisional Editorial Accept. SI accepted any file type with a recommended size below 10 MB and did not inherit the main-figure/table layout requirements. Wider supporting TIFFs were not treated as automatic failures. Native Excel recalculation, online NAAS and editorial-system upload were not performed.

Main tables remain in the manuscript; main figures and SI are separate uploads with matching item numbers and captions. The coauthors approved v2.0.0 and the scoped MIT/CC BY 4.0 grants. These grants cover author-owned contributions and do not override provider rights. Distinguish an author-drawn numerical plot from a copied provider image and from an underlying observation export. The real archive DOI and manuscript Data Availability insertion are still pending; see [publication status](../RELEASE_NOTES.md#publication-status). Any journal-required AI-assistance disclosure must describe actual use; no claim of human-only authorship or independent verification is made here.
