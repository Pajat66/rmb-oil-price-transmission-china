# Journal policy check

Official policies checked 2026-10-14: [data availability](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/data-availability), [Supporting Information](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/supporting-information), [figures](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/figures/), [licences and copyright](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/s/licenses-and-copyright).

The minimal dataset comprises observations needed for all findings plus methods/metadata. Entire unrelated raw archives are not required. If third-party data cannot be distributed, identify dataset/source, applicable permission, access contacts/procedure and known restrictions, so others can obtain it in the same manner. Neither a missing screenshot nor absence of every raw export automatically fails the policy. R4B-C2 closes the 2015–2016 Customs self-service access issue with independently checked official exports and a 24-month exact match. The v2.0.0 delivery now selects the documented official-provider access routes and a complete final Data Availability text. Actual archive creation, the real Version DOI and Word insertion remain publication steps. Packaging does not independently certify every contemporary source vintage or journal acceptance.

A direct repository data deposit must not use a more restrictive stated licence than CC BY. NBS conditional quoting is useful evidence but does not automatically satisfy that unrestricted-deposit route; third-party acquisition or specific clarification is the alternative.

Main figures: TIFF/EPS, 300–600 dpi, maximum 10 MB; at 300 dpi width 789–2250 and height at most 2625 pixels. All seven prepared TIFFs meet the measured format/dimension limits, including the approved author-original Figure 2. SI supports any file type and recommends less than 10 MB; SI does not follow the main figure/table requirements. The wider S1/S2 TIFFs are consequently not automatic failures. Item numbers and captions must correspond. Native Excel operation, NAAS and final editorial-system upload have not been performed.

PLOS publishes author works under CC BY-compatible terms and requires clearance of protected third-party content. Distinguish an independently drawn numerical scientific plot from a copied provider picture and from an underlying data export. The authors have approved v2.0.0, MIT software and CC BY 4.0 original graphics/tables/shareable results. Those decisions do not override provider rights; publication execution remains separately unauthorised.
