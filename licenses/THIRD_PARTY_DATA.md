# Third-party observations and rights exceptions

The author grants in LICENSE do not cover provider observations, full monthly derivatives combining those observations, third-party notices or dependencies. No author decision purports to grant upstream rights.

| Object | Included in v2.0.0 | Applicable boundary and access |
|---|---|---|
| FRED / Federal Reserve Board EXCHUS, 338-month numerical extract | data/analysis_ready/fred_exchus_monthly.csv | Upstream Public Domain: Citation Requested; retain Board/FRED/EXCHUS attribution. No author grant over FRED branding. |
| Historical EIA RBRTE export, with Thomson Reuters source credit | No | EIA public-domain/reuse policy contains a protected-private-contribution exception. A series-specific unrestricted redistribution grant is not asserted; obtain monthly RBRTE through DATA_SOURCES.md. |
| NBS monthly exports and complete computed observation tables | No | NBS permits research use and conditional attributed quotation/reproduction; unauthorized onward licensing is restricted. Obtain national monthly series by exact labels/date windows in DATA_SOURCES.md. Scope enquiries: info@stats.gov.cn. |
| Customs HS27090000 exports and complete monthly derivatives | No | Specific raw-file/derivative redistribution permission is not established. Official self-service acquisition for 2015–2016 and 24 exact monthly matches are already documented. Use the official platform; the service guide is a fallback for a specific unavailable vintage/permission question. |
| Complete mixed monthly Figure 2 source (7,774 records) | No | Obtain the individual observations, including outliers, from providers and apply the published transformations and sample masks. The author-created Figure 2 graphic is CC BY 4.0; the grant does not cover those inputs. |
| Third-party software, licence notices and fonts | No software/font binaries bundled | Keep DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.md and the historical MIT notice. External dependencies retain their own licences. |

No concrete conflicting institutional agreement or copied provider graphic was identified in the supplied material. Lack of a redistribution grant for excluded exports is not characterised as a proven prohibition of research use or of the original author graphics. Provider access, original-vintage identity and redistribution are distinct. Old commits and old releases can still contain earlier provider files because history is intentionally preserved; they receive no new author licence under v2.0.0.
