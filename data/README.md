# Necessary observations and their access

The selected v2.0.0 delivery is fixed: include the public-domain [FRED EXCHUS extract](analysis_ready/fred_exchus_monthly.csv), author code and shareable frozen statistical outputs; obtain the other provider observations through [specific official source procedures](../DATA_SOURCES.md). No author-only available-on-request promise is used. Upstream observations are not covered by the author licences.

| Necessary object | Contents and access |
|---|---|
| Long calendar, January 1998–February 2026 | 338 months: EIA RBRTE USD/barrel; FRED EXCHUS CNY/USD; national NBS CPI, PPI, total purchase-price and fuel/power purchase-price year-on-year indices; monthly and cumulative industrial activity. Exact labels, unit conventions and dates are in DATA_SOURCES.md. Preserve original missing values. |
| Customs calendar, January 2015–February 2026 | 134 months: imports, HS27090000, RMB yuan and first legal quantity kilograms. Official self-service exports match 24/24 months for 2015–2016 (48 exact integer comparisons). The other 110 months retain prior local-validation evidence and were not newly compared with official exports. The official monthly retrieval route is documented; no additional early-year application is a default requirement. |
| Transformations and samples | RMB oil cost = Brent × EXCHUS; 100 calendar log differences; inflation = year-on-year index minus 100; adjacent calendar differences, lag/lead eligibility, missing-value rules, S17 registry and separate p2/S18 samples. Import unit value = yuan/kg, quantity ten-thousand tonnes = kg/10^7, amount hundred-million yuan = yuan/10^8. Retained code/configuration and registries define the rules. |
| Figure 2 observations | Obtain the individual monthly values, coverage, transformed distributions, all displayed outliers and eligibility masks. The TIFF and summaries are not substitutes. See docs/FIGURE_DATA_ACCESS.md. The 7,774-row mixed observation source is not included. |
| Other figure/table evidence | Author-generated frozen results, bootstrap coefficient paths, plotted statistics, six table pairs and 21 SI files are included under CC BY 4.0 within their author-owned scope. |

The [raw-input contract](../config/raw_inputs.json) identifies ten historical source files and their checksums. A current provider export may have a different vintage/layout; document revisions rather than silently replacing the research version. Complete source-vintage reacquisition and end-to-end numerical/rendering reproduction were not newly tested by release preparation. No concrete necessary data object lacking an official access route was identified.

EIA historical source credit, NBS onward-licensing limits and unestablished Customs raw-file redistribution permission are explained in [third-party exceptions](../licenses/THIRD_PARTY_DATA.md). Where actual sharing restrictions apply, the official source and access/service contact are specified in [the final statement](../docs/DATA_AVAILABILITY_FINAL.md), rather than replaced by coauthor consent. Original exports, private evidence and complete mixed monthly inputs remain outside the v2.0.0 tree.
